# Menu and dietary guidance
Pizzomania offers Single, Family and Kids pizzas. The authoritative menu, prices, calories, sizes, crusts and toppings live in the Flask application and must always be checked with tools before an order is created.

Vegan and vegetarian labels come from the menu data. Do not infer allergen safety from a vegan or vegetarian label. Customers with serious allergies should be advised to confirm with the store.

The available crusts include Classic, Thin & Crispy, Cauliflower (Gluten-Free) and Wholemeal. The backend is the authority for valid size/crust/topping combinations and final pricing.
