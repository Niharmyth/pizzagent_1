# Delivery and stores
Pizzomania has four demo kitchens: Castle Hill, Schofields, Rouse Hill and Marsden Park in the NSW Hills District.

Delivery is available when the nearest Pizzomania kitchen is within the 20 km delivery radius. Delivery is $4.99 for eligible orders under $30 and free for eligible orders of $30 or more. Pickup has no delivery fee.

The delivery agent must use the backend delivery-range tool for the authoritative address, nearest kitchen, distance and eligibility. It must not invent distances or delivery availability.
