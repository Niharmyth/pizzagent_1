Frequently asked questions

Is {{ agent_name }} actually healthier than regular pizza?

We show calorie counts up front, offer vegan cheese and gluten-free bases, and tag lighter options as "Healthy Choice" so you can compare before you order — rather than guessing.

What if I'm not home for delivery?

This is a demo app, so there's no live courier contact — in a real version you'd be able to message your driver or leave delivery instructions here.

Can you cater for allergies?

Not yet in this demo — allergen filtering isn't built in. For a real order, always check with the store directly before ordering if you have a serious allergy.

Do you deliver outside the Hills District?

Delivery is available within 20km of your nearest of our 4 kitchens. Outside that range, we'll let you know and suggest pickup instead.

How does the loyalty program work?

Every order earns a point in your Account panel — every 5 points unlocks a free-pizza reward marker. It's demo-only for now and isn't deducted at checkout automatically.
